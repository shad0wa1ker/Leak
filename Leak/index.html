WHAT IS BROWSER FINGERPRINTING?

Browser fingerprinting is an information gathering technique that collects technical attributes from your browser and device environment. While individual attributes are commonplace, their unique combination creates a recognizable digital signature.

HOW IT WORKS 3 key concepts
Signal Aggregation Your screen alone matches 20% of users — add GPU, audio latency, fonts, and language list to narrow it down drastically.

Industry Use Banks use it to detect fraud and botnets. Ad networks use the same signals for cross-site behavioral tracking.
